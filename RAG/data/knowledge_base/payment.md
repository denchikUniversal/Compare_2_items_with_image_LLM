# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
DemoShop gift cards can be combined with any other payment method.
We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfers.

## Paying in installments

Orders over $100 can be paid in 4 interest-free installments with DemoShop Pay Later.
The first installment is charged at checkout and the rest every two weeks.
Missing an installment adds a $7 late fee.

## Promo codes

Only one promo code can be used per order.
Promo codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to items that are already discounted by more than 30%.
If you return part of an order that used a promo code, the refund is reduced by the share of the discount.

## Security

Card details are processed by our payment provider and are never stored on DemoShop servers.
For some orders your bank may ask for an extra confirmation through 3-D Secure.
