# Shoe Size Guide

## How to measure your foot

Stand on a sheet of paper in the evening, when your feet are the largest.
Mark the tip of your longest toe and the back of your heel, then measure the distance in centimeters.
If your feet have different lengths, choose the size for the longer foot.

## Brand fit notes

Nike running shoes tend to run small, so we recommend ordering half a size up.
Adidas Ultraboost fits true to size.
If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one; you can exchange shoes for another size for free within 30 days.
