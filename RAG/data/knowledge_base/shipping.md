# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options in the US

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99.
Standard shipping is free for orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99 for any order size.
Same-day delivery is available in New York, Chicago and San Francisco for $24.99 if you order before 11 AM local time.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union.
International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and costs a flat $29.99.
Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Order processing

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day are shipped the same day.
Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are shipped on the next business day.

## Tracking your order

You receive a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse.
You can also see the delivery status in "My orders" in your account.
If the tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, take photos of the box and the item and contact support within 48 hours.
We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including the shipping fee.
