# Warranty

## Standard warranty

All electronics sold by DemoShop come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
Vacuum cleaners and other home appliances have a 2-year manufacturer warranty.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as a battery that does not hold charge, broken buttons, or a device that does not turn on.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, cracked screens, or normal wear and tear.
Devices that were opened or repaired by an unauthorized service center lose their warranty.

## DemoShop Care extended protection

DemoShop Care extends the warranty to 3 years in total and adds coverage for accidental damage and cracked screens.
DemoShop Care costs 10% of the product price and can be added within 30 days after purchase.
Each DemoShop Care plan covers up to two accidental damage claims.

## How to make a warranty claim

Open "My orders", choose the product and click "Warranty claim".
Describe the problem and attach a photo or a short video.
We reply within 2 business days with a prepaid shipping label or the address of the nearest authorized service center.
Repairs usually take 10 to 14 business days; if the product cannot be repaired, we replace it.
