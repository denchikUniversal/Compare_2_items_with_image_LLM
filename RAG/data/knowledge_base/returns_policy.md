# Returns Policy

DemoShop wants you to love what you buy. If something is not right, you can return it.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Electronics, including headphones, phones, tablets and laptops, can be returned within 15 days of delivery.
Items bought during the Black Friday sale can be returned until January 15 of the following year.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.
Shoes must be returned in the original shoe box without signs of outdoor wear.
Opened electronics are accepted only if they are not damaged and all seals on accessories are intact.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, digital downloads and software license keys cannot be returned.
Underwear, swimwear and earbuds that were removed from a sealed hygiene pack cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
Personalized or engraved products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged.

## How to start a return

Open "My orders" in your account, choose the order and click "Return items".
Print the prepaid return label and drop the parcel at any partner pickup point.
Return shipping is free for orders within the US; international customers pay return shipping themselves.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the item.
If you paid with a gift card, the refund is added back to the gift card balance.
Original shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
